birthday-reminders

birthday-reminders is a small personal app that sends birthday reminder emails. It checks a list of birthdays and sends a reminder email from havatampa@gmail.com so upcoming birthdays are not missed.

How it works:

  • The app runs on a schedule and sends reminder emails only to havatampa@gmail.com.
  • It uses the Gmail API with only the gmail.send scope, which allows sending email. It does not read, delete, or modify other mailbox content.
  • It is for personal use by its owner only.

Data and privacy: the app does not sell or share personal data with anyone. It does not show advertising and does not track visitors to this page. See the Privacy Policy for details.
