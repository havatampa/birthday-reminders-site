Privacy Policy for birthday-reminders

Last updated: November 8, 2026

birthday-reminders is a small personal app that sends birthday reminder emails from havatampa@gmail.com. This policy explains what it does with information.

What the app does

The app checks a list of birthdays and sends a reminder email to havatampa@gmail.com when a birthday is coming up. It uses the Gmail API with only the gmail.send scope, which allows sending email. It does not read, delete, or modify other email or mailbox content.

Information collected

The app uses only the birthday list and email address needed to send the reminders, and only for that purpose. This website itself does not collect personal information from visitors, does not use cookies, and does not track visitors.

Sharing and selling data

We do not sell or share personal data with anyone. We do not share data with advertisers or other third parties. Data is not used for advertising.

Data retention

Reminder emails are sent as needed and are not kept by the app beyond what is needed to send them.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact havatampa@gmail.com.
